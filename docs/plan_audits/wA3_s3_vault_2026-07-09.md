## Incident caught by the verification (worth keeping)

`firehose_16.ndjson.4` (07-09, hour-16 retry segment) failed hop-1 MD5: the
initial sync excluded only the current hour's MAIN file, not its retry
segments, and .4 was mid-append when rsync copied it. **The three-hop check
caught the torn copy exactly as designed.** Fix: after hour 16 closed, the
whole day was re-manifested and re-synced excluding `firehose_17.ndjson*`
(main + segments) → BAD=0, S3 re-verify 84/84. Lesson encoded for W-A5's
delta sync: exclude `firehose_<current-hour>.ndjson*` (glob, not exact name).
