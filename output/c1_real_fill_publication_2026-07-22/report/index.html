Executive reading

  • Headline uses only strict-through: an at-price print never counts as our fill.
  • Future markout state must be strictly after the fill; visible exit quantity caps the executable component.
  • Censored quantity is not dropped: legal-price lower/upper bounds determine the verdict, with no fitted coverage threshold.
  • Queue-pessimistic and optimistic-at-touch are diagnostics, not promotion evidence.
  • No p-value is reported: three clean dates are shown independently, not treated as hundreds of thousands of independent trials.
  • A retain verdict can only request a 20-day validation; it cannot authorize live trading.
